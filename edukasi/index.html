Insurance literacy

Insurance Insights & Learning Center

Short summaries from trusted sources such as OJK (Indonesia’s Financial Services Authority), the media, and legal portals on how insurance works, what a policy contains, and how to prepare a claim, with links to the original articles.

01 • Insurance basics

Insurance Basics

What insurance is, how it works, and how it differs from saving.

Regulator

OJK describes insurance as a transfer of risk: the policyholder pays a relatively small premium, and the insurer pays a benefit or compensation if an insured event occurs. The material also includes a simple checklist for assessing your own and your family’s protection.

Source: OJK, Sikapi Uangmu · Insurance education material (PDF) · 2014

Open original source (new tab)
Regulator

Insurance is built on the principle of utmost good faith: the insurer must explain which risks the policy covers and which it does not, while the insured must disclose material facts honestly. The book also covers policyholders’ rights and obligations, from taking out a policy to making a claim.

Source: OJK, Sikapi Uangmu · “Lindungi Risiko Anda dengan Asuransi” (PDF) · September 2016

Open original source (new tab)
Media

Bisnis.com reports OJK’s explanation that insurance is different from savings: insurance protects against risk, while savings prepare funds for the long term.

Part of the article may only be available to subscribers.

Source: Bisnis.com · “Masyarakat Perlu Tahu, Asuransi Beda dengan Tabungan” · 29 June 2023

02 • Policy guide

Understanding Your Policy

Your rights as a policyholder, and what to check before and after you receive your policy.

Legal

Policyholders’ rights are also protected by Law Number 8 of 1999 on Consumer Protection, including the right to clear and accurate information about benefits, premiums, and the risks covered. Before buying, policyholders are advised to read the terms carefully and understand which risks are covered and which are not.

Source: Hukumonline · “Memastikan Iklim Asuransi yang Aman bagi Penanggung dan Tertanggung” · 11 September 2023

Open original source (new tab)
Legal

Insurers must hand over the policy to the policyholder, and the policy must set out the requirements and procedure for making a claim. The article also explains how to complain to OJK if the policy is never delivered.

This article refers to POJK 23/2015, which has since been revoked and replaced by POJK 8 of 2024.

Source: Klinik Hukumonline · “Sanksi Jika Polis Asuransi Tak Kunjung Diberikan” · 19 August 2020

03 • Claim tips

Preparing to File a Claim

Claim stages, the documents usually required, and common reasons claims are rejected.

Media

Kompas TV summarizes the claim stages from OJK’s website: the event is reported through the insurer’s official channels, and the insurer then assesses the claim against the policy terms and conditions. Documents usually requested include the policy, details of the loss, photos, and other supporting evidence.

Source: Kompas TV · “Pahami Tahapan Klaim Asuransi Agar Tak Gagal Saat Mengajukannya” · 10 January 2023
