How the Calculator Works

The protection gap is the difference between your estimated needs and the coverage you already have.

Life Insurance

Needs
Monthly family expenses × 12 × support period. If provided, plus outstanding debt, children’s education fund, and emergency fund & other costs.

Minus
Existing life coverage, plus savings & investments if provided.

Critical Illness

Needs
Monthly income × 12 × 3, 4, or 5 years (chosen in the additional details, 3 years by default), to cover treatment costs and replace income during recovery.

Minus
Existing critical illness benefit, if provided.
