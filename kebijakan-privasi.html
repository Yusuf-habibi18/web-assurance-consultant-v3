Privacy Policy

A simple explanation of what data we receive, what it is used for, and your rights over that data.

Last updated: [tanggal]

In short: this website does not store the data you enter. The form only composes a WhatsApp message. We only receive your data after you press send in WhatsApp yourself.

1. Who we are

This website is managed by Arits Fauzi Irham ("we"). Arits Fauzi Irham is an insurance agent (Tenaga Pemasar) of Manulife Indonesia. This website is managed personally and is not an official Manulife website. For privacy questions, contact us using the details in section 7.

2. Data we receive

We only receive the data you send us yourself via WhatsApp, namely:

  • your name;
  • your WhatsApp number;
  • the type of protection or topic you choose;
  • the message you write (if any);
  • a summary of your protection gap calculator results, if you choose to send it.

We do not ask for sensitive data such as your national ID number (NIK), policy number, bank account details, or medical history through the website. Please do not include them in the form.

3. How the form works

  1. The data you type is checked in your own browser and then composed into a message.
  2. The website opens WhatsApp with that message. At this stage, your data has not been sent to anyone.
  3. We only receive the data when you press send in WhatsApp.

This website has no database and does not store form entries.

The protection gap calculator works the same way. The numbers you enter are calculated in your browser and are not sent anywhere. We only receive a summary of the results (needs, existing coverage, and the gap) if you press the WhatsApp button on the calculator page and then press send.

4. Third-party services

  • WhatsApp (Meta): messages you send are processed under WhatsApp’s privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts: the website loads fonts from Google’s servers, so your device’s IP address may be visible to Google.
  • Hosting provider [nama penyedia]: like most web servers, the hosting provider may log technical visit data, such as IP addresses and browser types, for security and operational purposes.

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools.

5. How data is used

The data you send is used only to:

  • respond to your consultation request or questions;
  • contact you again regarding that request.

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone. [If your data is passed on to an insurance company for a policy application, this is done only with your consent. Konfirmasi dengan klien.]

6. Your rights

Under Law Number 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP), you have the right to:

  • know what data we hold about you;
  • request corrections to inaccurate data;
  • request deletion of your conversations and data from our records;
  • withdraw your consent and stop being contacted.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.
